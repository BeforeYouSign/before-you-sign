Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18th September 2016

Overview

Before You Sign ("we", "us") provides an automated review of building contracts uploaded by visitors to this website. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the site, how it's used, who else it's shared with, and how long we keep it.

What we collect

  • The document you upload — your building contract, and any supporting documents you add, which may contain your name, address, and details about your build.
  • Basic technical information — your IP address and browser information, collected automatically for security and to prevent abuse of the free scan.
  • Payment and contact details — if you purchase a review, payment is processed by our payment provider [Stripe — to be confirmed once payments are live]; we don't store your card details ourselves. If reviews are delivered by email, we collect the email address you provide for that purpose.

How we use it

  • To generate the free scan and, if purchased, the full review of your document
  • To process payment for paid reviews
  • To deliver your review, where applicable, by email
  • To protect the site from misuse (for example, limiting how many requests one visitor can make in a short period)

We don't use your uploaded documents for advertising, and we don't sell personal information to third parties.

Who we share it with

To provide the service, your document is processed by the following outside providers on our behalf:

  • Hosting and file storage — our website and the temporary storage used while your document is being processed are provided by Vercel Inc.
  • Document analysis — the text of your document is sent to a third-party document-processing technology provider (Anthropic) to generate your review. It is used only to produce your result and is not used to train that provider's models.
  • Payment processing — [Stripe, once payments are live] handles your payment details directly; we never see or store your full card number.

These providers are based overseas (primarily the United States), meaning your document is briefly transferred outside Australia during processing.

How long we keep it

Uploaded documents are stored only for as long as needed to complete your review, and are deleted from our storage once that's done. If you use the free scan but never purchase the full review, your file may remain in temporary storage for a short period before it is automatically cleared.

If reviews are delivered by email, a copy of your result may be retained in our email records for 12 months to handle support requests.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which involves a request to Google's servers when you load the page.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide, including encrypting data in transit and limiting how long documents are stored. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it, subject to any legal requirement we have to keep certain records. Contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example as our services or the law change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
